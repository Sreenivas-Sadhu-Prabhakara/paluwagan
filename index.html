The notebook that never
loses track of the turn.

A savings circle where everyone chips in each period and one member takes the pot — until it has gone all the way around. paluwagan keeps the paid marks, the payout order, and the trust, on your device and nowhere else.

  • Track the pot. Mark who paid each period, see the total collected and who still owes.
  • Rotate fairly. Set the payout order once; the schedule follows it to the last period.
  • Build trust. Each member earns an on-time rate — the trust in trust ledger.
